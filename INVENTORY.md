## 📝 Note on Course Materials

The course materials (4 system directories in /workspace/) are NOT included in this repository because:

1. They're large (~100+ MB)
2. They change frequently
3. They're licensed course content
4. They're provided through Vocareum or your course platform

**Your responsibility:** Obtain them from your instructor/platform and place in `/workspace/`

This repository provides the **harness** to test them (scripts, templates, guides).
